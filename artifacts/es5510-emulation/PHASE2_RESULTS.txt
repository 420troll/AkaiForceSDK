ORIGINAL ES5510 PROGRAMS
------------------------
Delay bundle: experimental/emulation/programs/original_stereo_feedback_delay.esp
  SHA-256 b3edb0490fc1bd7463d16476df8d8e7f20210f522e7953aafe8224eb14f3738f
  19 microinstruction words per frame; all delay, feedback, wet/dry mixing,
  MACs and stereo output execute in ES5510 instructions. GPR40=feedback,
  GPR41=wet, GPR42=dry (Q22). Default delay 250 ms, feedback 0.55, wet/dry
  0.5. Independent maximum bank default 10 s, total stereo ring 882,000 words
  at 44.1 kHz. MEMSIZ=0 (one address unit per 16-bit word).

Chorus bundle: experimental/emulation/programs/original_stereo_chorus.esp
  SHA-256 8d110bfc69c755eb05ea2f3af2cccdf8c41532a5eaf0c0f1ee7a6680948f66ad
48 kHz bundles (same program structures, sample-rate-scaled delay offsets):
  delay SHA-256 77049721e5a931b01eb3094534b28b27ab8ffd598562772a5676ecf1b2415a34
  chorus SHA-256 949f048bec6b14a829843b1aa07d9d7fdd0aa4af605079068b8970f1f5955429
  11 instruction words per frame; delay storage and audio mixing execute in
  the emulator. Two `lfo_gpr` bundle records define opposite-phase 0.5 Hz host
  sine updates to delay-offset GPR0/GPR1 (25 ms base, 5 ms depth); wet gain is
  GPR40 (0.5). The LFO schedule is renderer/host supplied, not ESP code.

Assembler: tools/es5510_asm.py. Readable source and parameter definitions:
  experimental/emulation/programs/stereo_feedback_delay.asm
  experimental/emulation/programs/stereo_chorus.asm
  experimental/emulation/programs/PHASE2_README.md
The assembler rejects stereo rings over the modeled 1M-word DRAM. A 10-second
per-channel bank fits at 44.1 and 48 kHz; it does not fit at 96 kHz.

VALIDATION
----------
PASS: native strict C++17 builds (-O2 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror) of
      engine smoke, source-derived vectors, renderer and benchmark.
PASS: original stereo-swap smoke and MAME pump fixture baseline reproduced.
PASS: deterministic MAME-source-derived ADD saturation/wrap, CCR, MAC and
      skip vectors (`experimental/emulation/mame_source_vector_test.cpp`).
      These are not a runtime MAME differential.
PASS: assembler regenerated both checked-in default bundles byte-for-byte.
PASS: regenerated all four final delay/chorus impulse/chord WAVs byte-for-byte.
PASS: repeated chorus render is identical; delay feedback stable; no clipping
      in the checked-in impulse/chord settings or tested parameter variants.
PASS: 48 kHz `_48k` delay/chorus bundles render 48,000 impulse frames, with
      12,000-frame delay and ~1,200-frame modulated chorus returns; no clipping.
PASS: 48 kHz 10-second stereo ring assembles (960,000 words); 96 kHz 10-second
      ring rejects because it exceeds 1M-word modeled DRAM.

MAME reference limitation: `/tmp/mame-es5510` is pinned to the commit above,
but is a sparse 5.2 MB source checkout without `src/emu`, build files or a MAME
executable. A test-only executing differential could not be built. Tests are
clearly marked source-derived vectors. See
`experimental/emulation/MAME_SOURCE_VECTORS.md`.

NATIVE PERFORMANCE — x86_64 INTEL CORE i7-10750H @ 2.60 GHz
------------------------------------------------------------
Optimized native benchmark, short=256 frames (median of 9), long=one second
(median of 3), including per-frame host-LFO writes for chorus:
  44.1 kHz: delay 19 words/frame, 0.5332 us/frame short / 0.5036 us/frame long.
             chorus 11 words/frame, 0.4047 / 0.4298 us/frame.
  48 kHz:   delay 19 words/frame, 0.4504 us/frame short / 0.5021 us/frame long.
             chorus 11 words/frame, 0.3234 / 0.3357 us/frame.
The 160-word no-op ceiling measured 4.49–5.29 us/frame on this desktop. No
x86 result is used as an ARM estimate. Full runs: `phase2/BENCHMARKS.md` and
`phase2/EFFECT_BENCHMARKS.csv`.
